About

I'm Nathalia, a Product Designer partnering with product teams across complex digital domains.

I design digital products where business goals, technology, and human behaviour intersect. I work across the entire product lifecycle, helping teams take products from 0 → 1, enhance existing experiences, and support innovation and continuous product growth.

I work closely with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to align teams, challenge assumptions, and make informed product decisions throughout the design process.

I believe great products should be accessible and usable by as many people as possible. No unnecessary complexity, just thoughtful design grounded in research, strategy, and close collaboration.
